CPR Online Module

1. Danger & Response

Before approaching, check for danger to yourself, bystanders and the casualty. Then check for a response — talk and touch (squeeze the shoulders).

2. Airway & Breathing

Open the airway with a head-tilt/chin-lift. Look, listen and feel for normal breathing for up to 10 seconds.

3. Compressions

If not breathing normally, start CPR: 30 compressions to 2 breaths, at a rate of 100–120 per minute, pressing one-third of chest depth.
